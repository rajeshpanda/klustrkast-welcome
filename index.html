Why riders Klustrkast

Made by a rider, for riders — every feature thought through, and the ones you'll actually reach for on every ride.

Seamless map navigation

A full, moving map right on your cluster — not a tiny arrow. Your roads, your style, day and night. Pick from the fastest and alternative routes before you set off.

Fuel stops on the map

Nearby pumps right on the cluster map, colour‑coded by brand with your preferred chains highlighted — plus a smooth fuel gauge that won't jump around.

Music & calls on the dash

Answer or wave off calls — phone and WhatsApp — from the handlebar. Skip tracks with album art, hands never leaving the grips.

Every ride, recorded

Speed, RPM, fuel and climb, saved automatically. Relive each ride in charts — then share a ride card straight to Instagram Stories, your stats baked in.

Make the dash yours

Set your own cluster wallpaper — crop any photo to the dash, or drop in an animated GIF that loops live on the screen.

Widgets on your dash

Pick what the cluster shows beside the map — RPM, voltage, fuel, range, altitude, economy and more, up to four live readouts, your choice.

For the best experience, bring your own API keys. KlustrKast uses MapTiler for maps and TomTom for routing and traffic. Both have free tiers — add your keys in Settings and the maps and ETAs get noticeably sharper. It still runs without them on open fallbacks.

Questions, answered

Will it void vehicle warranty?

No. The app doesn't make any permanent changes. All changes are stateless. For the vehicle it is same as being connected to its own app.

Are there ads?

KlustrKast is ad‑free — no ads, no tracking, ever. A small contribution covers upkeep (deployment, servers, development) and keeps it that way. The whole point is a seamless ride.

Which bikes does it work with?

Today, the TVS Apache RTX 300 Top / BTO (Oct 2025 onwards), the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V Top (Sept 2026 onwards) and the TVS Apache RTR 200 4V Top (Sept 2026 onwards). For the RTR 160 and RTR 200, it's the models with map mirroring over Wi‑Fi (the TFT cluster); the TFT models without map mirroring aren't supported. The RTR clusters have no music screen or joystick, so music shows on the map and calls are answered with the Info switch. RTR support is in beta while we test it on these bikes. The User Manual lists the differences. It's built on a shared cluster protocol, so more models can be added — tell us what you ride in the form.

Do I need any API keys?

For the best maps and routing, add your own MapTiler and TomTom keys in Settings — both offer free tiers. The app still works without them using open fallbacks; the keys just make maps and ETAs sharper.

Is there a user manual?

Yes — the KlustrKast User Manual covers setup, connecting to your cluster, navigation, rides, music and calls, widgets and troubleshooting.

Can I use it alongside my bike manufacturer’s app?

Not at the same time. Both apps try to control the cluster over the same Bluetooth link, so they send it conflicting instructions. Before you ride with KlustrKast, force stop the manufacturer’s app (long‑press its icon → App info → Force stop), or uninstall it if you don’t use it. Manufacturer apps may also run as a background service around the clock, which can drain your phone’s battery even on days you don’t ride. KlustrKast intentionally leaves that out: it runs only when you switch streaming on or start navigation, so you stay in control. The User Manual has the steps.

How do I get the app?

Tap “Get the app”, fill the form, and watch for the email with your download link. Access is contribution‑based at this time.

Is it on the Google Play Store?

No — KlustrKast is distributed as a direct Android APK, not through the Play Store. You'll install it yourself (sideload): open the file, and allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or files app when Android asks. Google Play Protect may show a warning for any app it hasn't seen before — tap “More details”, then “Install anyway”. The APK is signed, so every update verifies against the same signature.

Is there any warranty for the app?

No. KlustrKast is provided as‑is, with no warranty of any kind, and may contain bugs or show inaccurate or incomplete routes, maps, distances, ETAs or telemetry. Routes and information are suggestions only — not a guarantee that a road is safe, legal, open or passable. Always watch the road, follow traffic laws, and never interact with the app or the cluster while riding. You accept these terms when you register.

Is there an iOS variant?

No. But it is a work in progress.
