01What you need

  • A supported motorcycle with the map-mirroring (Wi‑Fi) cluster. See the supported bikes on the home page.
  • An Android phone running Android 12 or newer.
  • The Google account you registered with on the sign-up form.
  • Mobile data for planning routes and loading maps. Areas you've already ridden through are kept on the phone and load without signal.
  • Optional: your own free MapTiler and TomTom keys for sharper maps, traffic and better arrival times, or your own Google key if you prefer Google maps. See API keys.
Ride first. Set everything up while parked. Don't use the phone or the app while moving. Routes are suggestions, so always follow the road, signs and traffic laws.

02Install & sign in

KlustrKast isn't on the Play Store. You install it from the invite you receive by email.

  1. Open the invite email on your phone and follow its link to download KlustrKast.
  2. When Android asks, allow your browser or files app to Install unknown apps.
  3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. It warns about any app it hasn't seen before.
  4. Open KlustrKast and tap Sign in with Google. Choose the same account you registered with.

If you see a locked screen instead of the map, that account doesn't have access yet. Tap Sign out & try another account if you picked the wrong one, or reply to your invite email.

Updates arrive the same way. Install the new version over the old one and your rides and settings stay.

03First-time setup

Your name and birthday

On first launch, What's your name? asks for the name your cluster shows when your phone connects. Use up to 17 English letters, numbers and spaces. The screen previews exactly what the cluster will show. Tap Continue.

Add your Birthday (optional) (just the day and month) and the cluster wishes you a happy birthday on the day. Change either later in Settings → Rider name & birthday; the cluster picks it up the next time it connects.

Pick your motorcycle

Next, choose your bike under Which motorcycle? This matches the app to your cluster's turn arrows and call screen. You can change it later in Settings → My motorcycle.

Allow permissions

The Before your first ride screen lists what the app needs. Tap Allow … permission(s) (it shows how many are still missing) and accept each one. Tap Enable notification access if you want the extras below, then Start riding. Skip for now lets you do it later in Settings → Permissions.

PermissionWhat it's for
LocationPuts you on the map and follows your route.
BluetoothFinds your bike and drives the turn arrow on the cluster.
Nearby Wi‑FiSends the map picture to your cluster.
NotificationsKeeps the app running with your screen off.
PhoneShows who's calling, and lets the handlebar joystick (the Info switch on an RTR 4V) answer or reject.
Notification access (optional)Shows the song that's playing, handles WhatsApp calls, and mirrors Google Maps directions.

Let it run in the background

This is the setting that most affects screen-off riding. Open Settings → Permissions and tap Allow background activity. Without it, Android can pause music and live updates when the screen turns off.

The Permissions card only appears in Settings while something is still missing. If you don't see it, you're all set.

Stop your bike manufacturer's app

KlustrKast and your bike manufacturer's own app (the OEM app) can't both control the cluster at the same time. They share one Bluetooth link to the bike, and the manufacturer's app reconnects by itself in the background. With both running, the cluster gets conflicting instructions and KlustrKast may not connect or stay connected.

Before you ride with KlustrKast, do one of these:

  • Force stop it. Long-press the manufacturer's app icon, tap App info, then Force stop. It starts itself again when you open it or restart your phone, so repeat this when that happens.
  • Uninstall it if you don't use it. You can install it again later.

There's a second reason. Manufacturer apps may run as a background service around the clock: they start with your phone and keep looking for the bike, which can drain your battery even on days you don't ride. KlustrKast intentionally leaves that out. It runs only when you switch on Cluster streaming or start navigation, and it stops by itself a few minutes after the bike is switched off, so you stay in control.

04Detailed maps (MapTiler)

KlustrKast works straight away with free basic maps. For more detailed maps, with more streets, places and every map style, add your own MapTiler key. It's free, takes about two minutes, and you only do it once.

  1. In KlustrKast, open Settings, scroll to API keys and tap MapTiler key.
  2. Tap Get a key. MapTiler's website opens in your browser.
  3. Tap Sign in with Google and choose your Google account. The first time, this creates your free MapTiler account. No payment is needed.
  4. You land on MapTiler's API keys page. Press and hold the key (the long code under Key) and choose Copy link text. If no key is listed, tap New key, name it KlustrKast, save it, then copy it.
  5. Go back to KlustrKast, paste the key into the MapTiler key box and tap Save. The card now says Using your key — detailed MapTiler maps are on.
  6. Close and reopen the map (or switch streaming off and on) to load the new maps. Pick the look you like under Settings → Map style.
Keep your key to yourself. It's tied to your MapTiler account, and anyone who has it uses up your free allowance. KlustrKast keeps it on your phone only and hides it on screen; the eye icon shows it. Don't post screenshots with the key visible.

To go back to the free basic maps, clear the box and tap Save.

05Connect to your bike

  1. Turn the bike's ignition on. Make sure Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi are on in your phone.
  2. On the Map tab, switch on Cluster streaming at the top.
  3. The first time, Android asks whether to connect to the bike's Wi‑Fi. Accept it.
  4. Wait for the three moving dots to change to Active. The cluster then switches from its normal view to KlustrKast.
  5. Lock your phone and put it away. Streaming keeps running with the screen off.

Things to know

  • Your bike is remembered. The first cluster you connect to becomes your bike, and the app ignores other clusters nearby. That's Only connect to my motorcycle in Settings → My motorcycle, on by default.
  • Starting navigation switches streaming on for you. You don't have to flip the switch first.
  • The cluster shows the map only while you're navigating in KlustrKast. Otherwise it shows your idle wallpaper with your widgets and music.
  • If the link drops, the app reconnects on its own. After 5 minutes without finding the bike it stops and shows a Cluster not found notification. Tap it to try again.
  • To stop, switch Cluster streaming off. If you only turn the ignition off, the app looks for the bike for up to 5 minutes and then stops by itself.

Changing bikes? Go to Settings → My motorcycle → Forget this motorcycle, then connect to the new one.

06Navigate

Choose a destination

  • Search: type at least 3 letters in Where to? and pick a result. Recent places appear when you tap the box; tap the × on one to remove it, or long-press the × in the search box to clear them all.
  • Long-press the map: hold a spot briefly and release to drop a pin there.
  • Share from Google Maps: in Google Maps tap Share and choose KlustrKast. The place opens as a route preview.
  • Home and Office: tap the Home or Office chip under the search box.

Save Home and Office

Press and hold the spot on the map for 3 seconds. In Save this place, choose Home or Office. To change one later, hold the new spot and save again.

Pick a route and go

  1. The preview lists up to three routes: Fastest, then alternatives labelled by how much longer they take, such as +5 min. Each shows time, distance and arrival time.
  2. Tap a card to select that route. It's highlighted on the map.
  3. To change how this trip is planned, tap Normal or 2W above the routes. 2W avoids roads motorcycles aren't allowed on. The routes are planned again when you switch.
  4. Tap Start navigation.

If no MapTiler key is set, Start navigation first shows Add a MapTiler key. Paste your key and tap Save & start, or tap Continue without to ride on the basic map.

While riding

  • The phone shows the next turn, distance and road name at the top, with your speed and arrival time at the bottom. The cluster shows the map with the turn arrow and distance.
  • If you leave the route, it reroutes automatically.
  • With live traffic on, slow stretches of your route are drawn in amber, and jams or closed roads in red, on the phone and the cluster.
  • If you move the map by hand, tap Re-center to follow your position again.
  • When you arrive, the banner shows Location reached.

Voice guidance

While navigating, tap the speaker button to open Voice guidance, and under Announcements choose Off, Alerts (speaks once at the turn) or Full (an early heads-up plus a reminder at the turn). Picking Alerts or Full plays a short sample. It starts on Full. Voice works with the screen off and lowers your music while it speaks.

End navigation

Tap End in the app, or the End button on the navigation notification. Ending navigation doesn't disconnect the cluster. Switch Cluster streaming off for that.

Prefer Google Maps?

If you navigate in Google Maps while streaming, KlustrKast passes its turn arrow and distance to the cluster. This needs notification access, and the cluster shows the arrow without the map.

07Your cluster screen

These are under Settings → Cluster.

SettingWhat it does
Cluster streamFrame rate: Low (4 fps) to start with, Medium (9 fps) or High (15 fps). Higher is smoother but uses more battery and heat; it changes straight away. Map view: Speed-based (auto) to start with, which zooms out on highways and in near turns, or a fixed Zoom 13 to Zoom 17. Map tilt: 0° is flat, up to 60° for a 3D forward view (45° to start with).
Show road detailsIn the Cluster stream card. Off gives a distraction-free map with a plain arrow: no road name, turn distance or turn arrows.
Cluster widgetsUp to four live readouts beside the map, two per side: Widgets 1 and 2 on the right, 3 and 4 on the left. Choose from RPM, Voltage, Throttle %, Fuel % (beta), GPS Speed, Altitude, Fuel Economy (beta), Odometer and Range (km), or None. It starts with RPM and Voltage on the right.
Dark modeFor the cluster map only; the app and the phone map follow your phone's own dark mode. Auto (match bike) follows the bike's own day/night display, which the cluster's light sensor switches. Or choose Never, Forever, or Custom and set the From and To hours.
Idle wallpaperWhat the cluster shows when you're not navigating. Pick a photo and drag to frame it, or use an animated GIF.
  • A GIF wallpaper must already be 416 × 320 pixels. It plays as-is, and uses more battery than a still photo. To go easy on the cluster it always plays at Low (4 fps), whatever frame rate you picked in Cluster stream (the map keeps your setting).
  • Fuel % and Fuel Economy are marked beta, and Throttle % is still being verified, so treat those readings as approximate.

08Fuel stops

  • Any time: tap the fuel-pump button on the map to show petrol pumps on the phone and the cluster. Tap again to hide them. With a route active, it shows pumps ahead on your route.
  • Automatically when low: when the bike's fuel drops to the level you set, pump markers appear on the cluster by themselves.

Set this up in Settings → Fuel stations: Show pumps when low and its slider for the low-fuel level (30% to start with; the markers clear again once the tank is back above a higher level, shown under the switch), your Preferred brands (Bharat Petroleum to start with), and Always show on cluster if you want pumps visible on the cluster all the time. If none of your preferred brands are nearby, all pumps are shown so you're never stuck.

09Music & calls

Music

  • Use the handlebar music buttons to play, pause and skip tracks. They work with your screen off. (An RTR 4V has no music buttons or music screen: the song always shows on the map bar.)
  • With notification access granted, the buttons control whichever player is actually playing. Without it they still work, but may reach a different music app.
  • In Settings → Music, choose where the track shows: On the cluster (the bike's own music screen, map stays clear; the default) or On the map bar (along the bottom of the cluster map, needs notification access). The handlebar buttons work either way.

Calls

  • Incoming calls show on the cluster with the caller's name. Use the handlebar joystick to accept or reject (on an RTR 4V, the Info switch). The map stays up.
  • WhatsApp calls work too, and need notification access.
  • In Settings → Calls, choose On the cluster (joystick accept/reject; the default) or On the map bar (display only).

10Rides

Rides record automatically whenever you're navigating or streaming to the cluster, including when the cluster is just showing your wallpaper. There's nothing to start or stop: the ride is saved when your session ends. Very short trips (under about 500 m), and sessions where the bike stayed parked, aren't kept.

Look back at a ride

Open the Rides tab and tap a ride to see its route, distance, duration, speeds and elevation. With the cluster connected you also get odometer, fuel used, economy and RPM, plus charts you can tap to enlarge.

Manage rides

  • Rename: open a ride and tap the pencil. Settings → Ride tracking → Re-name existing rides by area names your saved rides after the area they were in.
  • Merge: long-press a ride, select the others, then tap Merge. The originals are kept.
  • Delete: tap the bin, then confirm.
  • Back up or move phones: on the Rides tab tap ⋮, then Export rides… to save a file, and Import rides… on the new phone.
Deleting is permanent. The app asks you to confirm before deleting a ride, and before Settings → Ride tracking → Clear ride history removes all of them. Export first if you want a copy.

Share a ride to Instagram

  1. Open a ride and tap the share icon.
  2. Shape the card with the four tabs: Image (add your own photo), Style (layout, font, Panel: Light, Glass or Map, and Story 9:16 or Square 1:1), Stats (which numbers to show) and Route (line colour).
  3. Drag to move and pinch to resize in the preview.
  4. Tap Instagram to open it as a Story (the button shows when Instagram is installed), or Download to save it to your photos.

Hide start & end (on by default, under Route) trims about 200 m off each end, so the card doesn't reveal your home or workplace.

To stop recording, turn off Settings → Ride tracking → Log my rides. Past rides stay.

11Widgets & shortcuts

Add these from your phone's home screen: long-press an empty area, choose Widgets, and find KlustrKast.

WidgetWhat it does
ControlOne button that switches cluster streaming on or off. Grey is off, orange is connecting, green is streaming.
QuickThe same streaming button, plus Home and Office. Those open the app and start navigating straight away on the fastest route.

You can also long-press the KlustrKast app icon for Navigate Home and Navigate to Office. Both need Home and Office saved first.

12Other settings

Most of these are under Settings → Navigation & maps.

SettingWhat it does
Map providerMapTiler + TomTom (the default) or Google, which uses your own Google key for maps, routes and search. See Google maps below. Reopen the map or restart streaming to apply.
Map styleThe look of the map on phone and cluster (Streets, Outdoor, Topo, Satellite and more). Needs a MapTiler key; without one the app uses a basic free map. A dark version switches in at night; styles marked * (Satellite and Hybrid) have none, so they stay light at night. With the Google provider the choices are Roadmap, Satellite * and Hybrid (satellite) *. Reopen the map or restart streaming to apply. Satellite styles use more data.
Live trafficNeeds a TomTom key, or Google as the map provider. Slow stretches of your route are drawn in amber, and jams or closed roads in red. Traffic is counted in your arrival time, which is updated every 20 minutes while you navigate. Your route and turn instructions don't change. Turn Show traffic off to plan the route once and keep it as it is.
Route guidanceNormal guidance is quicker to plan and includes lane guidance. 2W guidance avoids roads motorcycles aren't allowed on, such as some expressways. Switch to 2W only if Normal sends you down a road you can't ride. It applies to the next route you plan (or the next reroute). You can also switch for a single trip on the route preview.
Location sourceLeave on Fused + GPS (recommended). Try GPS only if the map jumps; try Fused + GPS + Network only if GPS won't lock on your phone. Fused only is the lightest on battery but can lag. Applies the next time navigation or streaming starts.
Fuel stationsSee Fuel stops.
Map cacheAreas you ride through are stored (up to 400 MB, oldest dropped first) so they load without signal. Clear map cache frees the space.
AccountAt the bottom of Settings: who's signed in and the app version. Logout signs you out.

API keys

Optional. The API keys section has a card for each key: paste it, tap Save, or tap Get a key to open the provider's site. Keys are stored on this phone only. Reopen the map or restart streaming to apply. The app works without them, on free open maps and routing.

KeyWhat it gives you
MapTiler keyDetailed maps and the Map style choices. Free at cloud.maptiler.com (step-by-step guide). Without it, maps use the basic OpenFreeMap tiles.
TomTom keyTomTom routing, live traffic and richer place search. Free at developer.tomtom.com (20,000 requests a month). Without it, or when it runs out, routing falls back to free open routers and search to OpenStreetMap.
Google Maps keyLets you choose Google under Map provider. See below.

Google maps (optional)

If you prefer Google's maps, you can use your own Google Maps Platform key. Google bills your Google account for every map tile, route and search, so check their pricing first.

  1. In Google Cloud, turn on Map Tiles API, Routes API and Places API (New), with billing. Restrict the key to those APIs (not to an Android app).
  2. In Settings → API keys → Google Maps key, paste the key and tap Save.
  3. In Settings → Map provider, choose Google. Reopen the map or restart streaming.
  • You get Google map tiles on the cluster and the phone, Google routes (Normal is a car route, 2W a two-wheeler route) with traffic, and Google place search. Google routes have no lane arrows.
  • If Google refuses the key (wrong key, an API not turned on, or billing), the app tells you and uses MapTiler + TomTom until you change the key or the provider. If a single Google route or search fails, that one request uses MapTiler + TomTom instead.
  • Clearing the Google key puts the provider back to MapTiler + TomTom.

Metrics

The Metrics section counts the requests this phone makes with each of your keys, Today and This month, with a card for each provider you have a key for (Google, TomTom, MapTiler). Use it to keep an eye on free limits and on Google charges. Map tiles loaded from the cache aren't counted (or billed). Days and months follow your phone's clock, and your provider's own console is the real bill.

13Troubleshooting

The cluster won't connect
  1. Check the ignition is on, and that both Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi are on in your phone.
  2. On Android 13 or newer, check Nearby Wi‑Fi is allowed (Settings → Permissions).
  3. If Android asked to connect to the bike's Wi‑Fi and you declined, switch streaming off and on, and accept it.
  4. Turn the ignition off, wait a few seconds, turn it on, and switch streaming on again.
  5. Force stop your bike manufacturer's app, or uninstall it. It competes with KlustrKast for the cluster (see First-time setup).
  6. New or different bike? Use Settings → My motorcycle → Forget this motorcycle.
The app keeps trying for 5 minutes before it stops.
It works, but stops when I lock the phone
Allow background activity: Settings → Permissions → Allow background activity. Also check your phone's own battery settings for KlustrKast and set them to Unrestricted, and turn off any power-saving mode while riding. Make sure Notifications are allowed.
The cluster shows my wallpaper, not the map
That's expected when you aren't navigating. Start a route in KlustrKast and the map appears.
The map is blank and only my route shows
  1. Open Settings → API keys → MapTiler key. Tap the eye icon and check the key matches the one on MapTiler's API keys page, with no spaces before or after it. Paste it again and tap Save.
  2. Close and reopen the map.
  3. If you added limits to the key on MapTiler (allowed websites or User-Agent), remove them. They can block the app.
  4. Still blank? Clear the key and tap Save to use the free basic maps, then tell us.
The map jumps, or my position lags
Update to the latest version first. Keep Location source on Fused + GPS, and check the phone isn't in a power-saving mode. If it still happens, send us a ride export (below).
Live traffic is on, but my route is all blue
That's normal most of the time. Only stretches where traffic actually adds a delay, and closed roads, are coloured, so a route with moving traffic stays blue. Colour is drawn on the route you've selected, so in the preview tap another route card to see its jams. Check that a TomTom key is saved in Settings → API keys (or that Google is your map provider) and that Show traffic is on in Settings → Live traffic.
The map style doesn't change, or the map looks basic
Map styles need a MapTiler key: add one in Settings → API keys → MapTiler key. After changing the style or a key, reopen the map or switch streaming off and on.
Google maps stopped, or the app says Google refused the key
The app has switched back to MapTiler + TomTom for now. In Google Cloud, check that Map Tiles API, Routes API and Places API (New) are turned on, that billing is set up, and that the key isn't restricted to an Android app. Then save the key again in Settings → API keys → Google Maps key, which gives Google another try.
No song title on the cluster, or the buttons control the wrong app
Grant notification access: Settings → Permissions → Enable notification access.
The joystick doesn't answer calls
Set Settings → Calls to On the cluster and make sure the phone/calls permission is allowed. For WhatsApp calls, notification access is needed as well. On an RTR 4V there's no joystick: use the Info switch on the left of the handlebar.
"No GPS fix yet" or "Couldn't build a route"
For GPS, step outside into the open and wait a few seconds. Routes need mobile data, so check your connection and try again.
The app says this version is no longer supported
Install the latest version from your invite email. Your rides and settings are kept.
How do I report a problem?
On the Rides tab tap ⋮ → Export rides… and send us the file, with your phone model, your bike, and what happened. The export includes a small technical summary for each ride (update rate, battery, power-saving), which helps us find the cause. Reply to your invite email, or message @geargyani on Instagram.

14Good to know

  • The app keeps your latest 100 rides. Older ones are removed automatically, so export if you want to keep everything.
  • Pressing Back on the Map tab only hides the app. Streaming and navigation keep running.
  • KlustrKast only displays information on the cluster. It doesn't change anything on the bike.
  • There are no ads. Your rides are stored on your phone, not on our servers. The app does send crash and connection-error reports so problems can be found and fixed.
  • API keys you add stay on your phone. Requests made with them count against your own account with that provider, and Google charges your account for what you use.
  • KlustrKast is provided as-is, with no warranty. See the FAQ on the home page for the full terms.

15RTR 4V riders

KlustrKast supports the TVS Apache RTR 160 4V Top and RTR 200 4V Top with the TFT cluster that has map mirroring over Wi‑Fi. The TFT models without map mirroring (Bluetooth only) aren't supported. Everything in this manual applies, with the differences below.

RTR support is in beta. It's built from how the TVS app talks to these clusters and is still being tested on the bikes. If something doesn't work the way this section describes, send us a DM on Instagram (@geargyani).

Set up

  • Pick TVS RTR 160 4V TOP or TVS RTR 200 4V TOP under Settings → My motorcycle. This sets the right turn arrows, turn text and call screen for your cluster.
  • Your cluster's Bluetooth name starts with TVS followed by letters and numbers (for example TVSEKA002). It shows on the speedometer when you switch the ignition on.
  • As with every bike, stop the TVS app before you ride with KlustrKast.

What's different on the RTR 4V

FeatureOn the RTR 4V
MusicThe cluster has no music screen, so the song and artist always show on the map bar along the bottom of the cluster. Settings → Music is fixed to On the map bar, and needs notification access to show the song. No album art is sent.
Handlebar buttonsThere's no joystick. The Info switch (left side) accepts, rejects and ends calls. The Mode switch (right side) changes the ride mode on the bike; KlustrKast doesn't use it. There are no music buttons, so control music from your phone or headset.
CallsWork as on the RTX: the caller's name shows on the cluster and the map stays up. Choose On the cluster in Settings → Calls to answer with the Info switch.
Turn textNext to the turn arrow the cluster shows its own short lines, such as staying on the same road or the turn direction close to the turn. That's how this cluster displays directions.
Low fuelKlustrKast doesn't use the cluster's "hold Info to navigate to the nearest fuel station" prompt. Pump markers still appear on the cluster map when fuel drops to your low-fuel level, as described in Fuel stops.
Day and nightWith Dark mode on Auto, the map follows the cluster's own day and night themes, which its light sensor switches. Changing ride mode (Urban, Sport, Rain) doesn't change it.
Voice assistNot supported by KlustrKast yet.

Works the same

Map mirroring, navigation and rerouting, voice guidance, live traffic, cluster widgets (speed, RPM, fuel, range and the rest), fuel stops, ride tracking and sharing.

RTR 4V support is new. If something doesn't behave as described here, tell us with your bike model and what happened.
